# PRISM — Pricing Intelligence for Dubai Brokerages

*A pitch one-pager for small and mid-size brokerages (5–50 agents)*

---

## The credibility hook

PRISM didn't start as a SaaS idea. It started as a 75-page academic dissertation analyzing **547,545 residential transactions** registered with the Dubai Land Department between 2020 and 2025, using a hedonic pricing model (the same econometric framework real estate economists use to isolate the dollar value of any single attribute — brand, view, floor, size — while holding everything else constant).

The headline finding: **branding is not one thing.**

| Tier | What it is | Premium vs. non-branded (controlled) |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 — Lifestyle brands | Vida, contemporary-branded | **–6,080 AED/sqft** (a discount, p=0.039) |
| Tier 2 — Premium hotel brands | Address-type, hotel-operated | **+7,050 AED/sqft** (stable, lowest volatility) |
| Tier 3A — Designer/architectural | Armani, Elie Saab, Bvlgari | **+20,696 AED/sqft** |
| Tier 3B — Ultra-luxury hospitality | Four Seasons, St. Regis, Palace | **+20,559 AED/sqft** |

The market doesn't pay for a logo. It pays for **operational depth** — service guarantees, rental-pool access, institutional management. A "lifestyle" brand with no operational substance behind it actually *underperforms* an unbranded prime unit once location and developer are controlled for. A brand backed by a global hotel operator commands a premium that survives even the COVID shock.

That's not an opinion. It's a regression result with p < 0.001 and a sample of half a million transactions. **PRISM is that finding, turned into software that runs on live data instead of a 2020–2025 snapshot.**

## What PRISM actually does today

- **Pricing Proof** — agent enters a listing (area, project, size, asking price) → PRISM scores it against live DLD comparables and returns a verdict (Strong Buy / Fair Value / Overpriced), a fair-value estimate, a P25–P75 comparable band, and an AI-generated analyst note the agent can send straight to a client or post as content.
- **Market Pulse** — live median AED/sqft by community, trend direction, off-plan share — refreshed from DLD, not a quarterly PDF.
- **New-launch detection** — flags projects whose *first-ever* DLD transaction appeared in the last 30 days, often before they're live on the portals.
- **Off-plan intelligence** — every curated listing enriched with the DLD median for its community, so the brochure number sits next to the real number.
- **Content engine** — every Pricing Proof run can generate a ready-to-post social caption and a WhatsApp-ready client message. The tool that proves the deal also produces the content that markets the agent.

This is the same DLD dataset the dissertation used — just live, queried in real time, instead of pulled once for a methodology chapter.

## Why a small brokerage needs this (and can't build it)

Big franchises (Betterhomes, Allsopp & Allsopp, Provident) already run internal data teams and publish their own market reports. A 10–30 agent brokerage competes against that with WhatsApp PDFs and "trust me, I've sold here for years." That pitch is losing ground.

**Buyer mentality in Dubai is shifting from qualitative to quantitative.** Buyers — especially the international and HNWI segment the dissertation studied — increasingly want to see the comparable, not just hear the confidence. A broker who can pull up a live DLD-backed verdict in front of a client, on the spot, wins the room that a broker reciting "the market is hot" does not.

PRISM is the fastest way for a small brokerage to look — and actually be — as data-literate as the biggest names in the market, without hiring a data scientist or an engineering team.

## Commercial options

**1. White-label SaaS license (recommended)**
- Brokerage gets a branded instance of PRISM (their logo, their agents' WhatsApp links, their off-plan inventory).
- Monthly or annual license fee per brokerage, scaled by agent seat count.
- I retain ownership and keep shipping improvements (new endpoints, better AI commentary, more data sources) across every licensee — brokerages get upgrades for free as part of the subscription.
- Lowest upfront cost for the brokerage, recurring revenue for me, and a defensible moat: the product gets better for everyone every time one client asks for a feature.

**2. Outright purchase (IP transfer)**
- One-time price for the full codebase, the dissertation-derived methodology, and a transition/training period.
- Makes sense only for a buyer who wants to own and operate this as their core differentiator long-term and has the technical capacity to maintain it.
- Priced at a premium to the SaaS option to reflect the lost recurring revenue and the loss of control over future direction.

**3. Revenue share / embedded placement**
- PRISM's Pricing Proof and content engine embedded directly into the brokerage's existing site or CRM.
- No license fee; instead, a percentage of closed deals sourced through PRISM-attributed leads, or a per-lead fee.
- Lowest commitment way for a skeptical brokerage to test the tool before committing to a license.

**Recommendation: lead with option 1.** It's the lowest-friction "yes" for a brokerage owner, it builds a recurring book of business instead of a single payday, and every brokerage that signs on makes the product (and the pitch) stronger for the next one.
