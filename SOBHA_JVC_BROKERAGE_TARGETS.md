# Sobha × JVC — Brokerage Target List

Brief: find brokerages in JVC with an off-plan focus and a large team, to tap into for Sobha
distribution.

Researched August 2026. Every headcount below is a **claimed** figure from a company or
directory source unless marked otherwise — see [Verify before you pitch](#verify-before-you-pitch)
for the authoritative check.

---

## Read this first — the brief needs one adjustment

Two things surfaced in research that change how the list should be used.

**1. Almost nobody large is actually headquartered in JVC.**
JVC is a residential community. Its entire commercial office stock is essentially
Prime Business Centre (two G+17 towers, ~85 offices each) plus office floors inside a few
mixed-use residential towers such as Regina Tower (18 offices). The 120+ "JVC agencies" that
directories list are overwhelmingly one-to-ten-agent shops squeezed into that stock, or firms
tagged to JVC because they *list* there. Firms with genuinely large teams cover JVC from a
branch or a JVC desk, with the HQ in Business Bay, Barsha Heights, Motor City or JLT.

So "large team + JVC head office + off-plan" is close to an empty set. "Large team + off-plan +
owns JVC volume" is a real and useful set. The list below is organised that way.

**2. Price-band fit is the actual qualifier, not location.**
JVC has been Dubai's number-one community by transaction volume for three years running —
roughly 18,745 homes traded in 2025, more than Dubai Marina, Business Bay and Downtown — but
at an average ticket around AED 880k. Sobha's Dubai product sits well above that band.

A pure JVC secondary shop has a buyer book of AED 600k–1.2m studio and 1BR investors. That book
does not convert to Sobha Hartland II or Sobha Central. The JVC firms worth your time are the
ones where JVC volume is a **lead-generation engine** feeding an off-plan desk that sells across
price bands — not the ones where JVC *is* the business.

Screen every name below on that question before you spend a meeting on it.

## Verify before you pitch

Every headcount above is self-reported. Website and directory numbers routinely count
administrative staff, inactive licences and overseas contractors.

The authoritative source is the **DLD broker register** published on Dubai Pulse: every licensed
broker (BRN) is tied to a brokerage (ORN), so counting brokers per ORN gives you a real,
government-recorded team size — and filtering to currently valid licences separates a genuine
80-agent floor from an 80-name CRM.

`prism-valuation-web-mvp/scripts/broker-targets.js` does this. It pulls the broker and office
registers, ranks brokerages by active registered agents, joins office contact details, and flags
JVC-located offices.

```bash
cd prism-valuation-web-mvp
DUBAI_PULSE_API_KEY=… \
DUBAI_PULSE_BROKERS_RESOURCE_ID=… \
DUBAI_PULSE_OFFICES_RESOURCE_ID=… \
npm run broker-targets
```

Outputs `out/broker-targets.md` and `out/broker-targets.csv`.

Two caveats worth knowing before you rely on it:

- **The register gives you team size, not off-plan focus.** DLD does not tag brokerages by
  off-plan versus secondary, and the transactions dataset carries no broker field. Off-plan focus
  still has to come from portal listing mix or from asking them directly.
- **Office location may not be a register field.** If the offices dataset carries no address or
  area column, the script falls back to matching the seed list of JVC firms in this document.
  It prints the detected schema on every run so you can see which path it took.

Run it from your machine — this environment's network policy blocks `dubaipulse.gov.ae` and the
property portals, which is why the list above is search-sourced rather than register-sourced.

## Sobha context for the pitch

- 2026 lineup spans Sobha Hartland II, Motor City, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubailand, Dubai Harbour
  and Sobha Siniya Island (Umm Al Quwain) — entry-level apartments through ultra-premium
  waterfront, so there is genuinely something for a mid-market desk to sell.
- Standard payment structure is 60/40, with apartment launches typically 10% on booking and
  villa launches 10–20% on booking with 40–50% spread across construction.
- Transaction costs: 4% DLD registration plus roughly 2% brokerage.
- Sobha reports a record 6,819 units set for handover in 2026, and has expanded into Abu Dhabi
  with a 38m sq ft waterfront community — a delivery track record that is the strongest single
  talking point with a brokerage that has been burned by a developer missing handover.
- Channel partner registration runs through Sobha directly: Sobha Sapphire, Ground Floor,
  Al Khail Road, Business Bay; marketing@sobha.com.
