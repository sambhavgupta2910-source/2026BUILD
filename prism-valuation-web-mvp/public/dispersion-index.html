The Elevate Price Dispersion Index

Every month we measure something brokers don't publish: how much registered sale prices for the same home type, in the same community, actually vary. A wide spread means the market is pricing on guesswork. A tight spread means the comparable evidence is doing its job. This is computed directly from DLD records — no scraped listings, no asking prices, nothing we can't prove.

How this is calculated

For each community + room-count cohort with at least 8 registered sales in the trailing window, we take the 25th and 75th percentile of AED/sqft among those sales and express the gap as a percentage of the median. That percentage is the dispersion score — the same diagnostic PRISM's valuation engine reports on every individual deal check, just aggregated across the whole market instead of one listing. Higher dispersion means buyers and sellers in that cohort are agreeing on price far less than the data says they should.

This is an analytical reading of registered DLD transactions, not a rating of any brokerage, agent, or named project. Methodology is actively being refined — see the investor deck for the full PRISM engine notes.
